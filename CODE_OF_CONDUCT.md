# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MyPet to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for all people, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, inclusive and healthy
community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that help:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially with newcomers
- Respecting differing opinions, experiences and skill levels
- Giving constructive feedback and accepting it gracefully
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information (such as a physical or email address)
  without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any place where someone officially represents the project.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. The maintainer
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@eramitgupta](https://github.com/eramitgupta), through the contact options on their GitHub
profile. Don't post the details in a public issue. All reports are reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter are respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer follows these guidelines when deciding on consequences:

1. **Correction** — for inappropriate language or other minor unprofessional behaviour: a
   private, written warning that explains the problem. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — for a single serious incident or a pattern of minor ones: a warning with
   consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a
   period of time.
3. **Temporary ban** — for a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour:
   a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression
   toward or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from the project.
