# Changelog

All notable changes to MyPet are listed here. Each release's section is shown in the in-app
update window, so write it for users: what's new and what got better.

## [0.0.2] - 2026-11-09

### Smoother running

- Fixed a glitch where the running pet's legs sometimes merged into one. The dog, cat, fox and
  panda now keep their front and back legs clearly apart in every step.
- The pet menu now says **Settings** and **Check for Updates** in full, without being cut off.

## [0.0.1] - 2026-10-21

### First release 🐈

- Six pixel-art pets (cat, dog, fox, rabbit, dinosaur, panda) that live in your menu bar.
- The pet's colour follows your Mac's memory, and its speed follows the CPU.
- Choose between **RAM usage** and **memory pressure**, and pick your own colours.
- Flip your pet, or let it turn around on its own.
- Battery-friendly: it stops animating when the menu bar is hidden and slows down on battery.
- **Check for Updates…** keeps MyPet up to date automatically.
